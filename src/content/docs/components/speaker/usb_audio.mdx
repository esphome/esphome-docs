The `usb_audio` speaker platform plays audio through the output of a USB Audio Class device, such as a USB
speakerphone, headset or sound card, connected through the [USB Audio](/components/usb_audio/) component.

This platform only works on the ESP32-S2, ESP32-S3, ESP32-S31, ESP32-P4 and ESP32-H4 with the
[ESP-IDF framework](/components/esp32/#framework).

> [!WARNING]
> Audio and voice components consume a significant amount of resources (RAM, CPU) on the device.
>
> **Crashes are likely to occur** if you include too many additional components in your device's
> configuration. In particular, Bluetooth/BLE components are known to cause issues when used in
> combination with Voice Assistant and/or other audio components.

```yaml
# Example configuration entry
speaker:
  - platform: usb_audio
    id: usb_speaker
```

## Configuration Variables

- **usb_audio_id** (*Optional*, [ID](/guides/configuration-types#id)): The ID of the
  [USB Audio](/components/usb_audio/) component to use. Only needed when more than one is configured.
- **sample_rate** (*Optional*, positive integer): The sample rate of the stream, from `8000` to `96000`.
  Defaults to `48000`.
- **bits_per_sample** (*Optional*, enum): The bit depth of the audio samples. One of `8bit`, `16bit`, `24bit` or
  `32bit`. Defaults to `16bit`.
- **num_channels** (*Optional*, positive integer): The number of channels, `1` or `2`. With `2`, each sample of the
  first channel is followed by a sample of the second channel. Defaults to `2`.
- **buffer_size** (*Optional*, positive integer): The size in bytes of the USB Audio Class driver's ring buffer for
  this stream. Defaults to `6400`.
- **write_timeout** (*Optional*, [Time](/guides/configuration-types#time)): The longest time a single write waits
  for room in the buffer before it returns, so the component sending audio can try again. Defaults to `20ms`.
- All other options from [Speaker Component](/components/speaker/#base-speaker-configuration).

The device must support the configured `sample_rate`, `bits_per_sample` and `num_channels` exactly, and the speaker
only accepts audio in that format. If the device does not support the format, the formats it supports are logged
when it is plugged in; see [Audio Format](/components/usb_audio/#audio-format).

The [Speaker Audio Media Player](/components/media_player/speaker/) converts its audio to the speaker's format, and a
[Mixer Speaker](/components/speaker/mixer/) or a [Resampler Speaker](/components/speaker/resampler/) in front of this
speaker can convert audio from other sources. [Voice Assistant](/components/voice_assistant/) can play its
responses through the speaker directly with its `speaker` option, or through a media player.

Volume and mute are sent to the device as USB Audio Class controls just before a media player pipeline writes its
next audio, so a change made while nothing is playing takes effect when playback starts. Components that write
audio from the main loop, such as [RTTTL](/components/rtttl/), leave the device's own volume setting untouched. If
the device does not support a control, a warning is logged and that control has no effect. The settings are sent
again when the device reconnects.

When the device is unplugged while playing, playback stops. The speaker starts again with the next audio it is asked
to play, once the device is plugged in.

## Example

```yaml
speaker:
  - platform: usb_audio
    id: usb_speaker

  - platform: mixer
    output_speaker: usb_speaker
    source_speakers:
      - id: announcement_speaker
      - id: media_speaker

media_player:
  - platform: speaker
    name: "USB Speaker"
    announcement_pipeline:
      speaker: announcement_speaker
    media_pipeline:
      speaker: media_speaker
```
