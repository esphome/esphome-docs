description: "Play audio on a single GPIO pin with the ESP32 sigma-delta modulator and a simple RC filter."
title: "Sigma-Delta Speaker"
---

The `sigma_delta` speaker platform plays audio on a single GPIO pin using the sigma-delta modulator
built into the ESP32. A resistor and a capacitor on the pin turn the pulse stream into an analog
audio signal. No external DAC or I²S codec is needed.

The output is 8-bit, so expect a signal-to-noise ratio of roughly 30 to 40 dB. This is good enough
for voice prompts, alerts and casual listening, but it is not suitable for high-quality music playback.

This platform only works on ESP32-based chips with a sigma-delta modulator (all variants except the
ESP32-C2 and ESP32-H2) using the [ESP-IDF framework](/components/esp32/#framework).

> [!WARNING]
> Audio and voice components consume a significant amount of resources (RAM, CPU) on the device.
>
> **Crashes are likely to occur** if you include too many additional components in your device's
> configuration. In particular, Bluetooth/BLE components are known to cause issues when used in
> combination with Voice Assistant and/or other audio components.

```yaml
# Example configuration entry
speaker:
  - platform: sigma_delta
    id: sigma_delta_speaker
    pin: GPIOXX
```

## Configuration Variables

- **pin** (**Required**, [Pin Schema](/guides/configuration-types/#pin-schema)): The output GPIO pin
  that carries the audio signal. The `inverted` pin option is supported.

- **sample_rate** (*Optional*, positive integer): The sample rate the speaker plays at. Must be
  between `8000` and `48000`. Audio at other sample rates is resampled to this rate by the audio
  pipeline. Defaults to `44100`.

- All other options from [Speaker Component](/components/speaker/#config-speaker).

Any 8 to 32 bit mono or stereo audio stream is accepted. Stereo audio is mixed down to mono, and all
audio is converted to the 8-bit pulse density used by the modulator, which runs at a fixed 1 MHz.
Volume and mute are applied in software.

## Hardware Setup

An RC low-pass filter between the pin and the amplifier removes the high-frequency pulses and leaves
the audio signal:

```text
GPIOXX --[1 kΩ]--+--> amplifier / headphones / speaker
                 |
               [10 nF]
                 |
                GND
```

- A 1 kΩ resistor with a 10 nF capacitor gives a cutoff frequency of about 16 kHz.
- For a softer sound use a 22 nF capacitor (cutoff about 7 kHz). For a brighter sound use a 4.7 nF
  capacitor (cutoff about 34 kHz).
- For headphones, add a 100 Ω series resistor and a 10 µF capacitor in series to block DC.
- A GPIO pin cannot drive a passive speaker directly. Connect the filter output to an amplifier
  module for a speaker.

Any output-capable GPIO pin can be used. Avoid strapping pins and pins already used by other hardware
on the board:

| Board                     | Tested pin      | Note                                                  |
| ------------------------- | --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| ESP32-C3 SuperMini / Zero | GPIO1           | GPIO2 also works. Do not use strapping pins 8 and 9.  |
| ESP32-S3-BOX-3            | GPIO1 or GPIO14 | Do not use the display or touch pins (GPIO4/5/6/8).   |
| ESP32 (original)          | Any output GPIO |                                                       |

## Media Player Example

A [Speaker Source Media Player](/components/media_player/speaker_source/) can feed the speaker, for
example to play music streamed with [Sendspin](/components/media_source/sendspin/):

```yaml
sendspin:
  id: sendspin_hub

speaker:
  - platform: sigma_delta
    id: sigma_delta_speaker
    pin: GPIOXX

media_source:
  - platform: sendspin
    id: sendspin_source
    buffer_size: 40000  # Smaller buffer for boards without PSRAM

media_player:
  - platform: speaker_source
    id: main_player
    media_pipeline:
      speaker: sigma_delta_speaker
      sources:
        - sendspin_source
```

On boards without PSRAM, such as most ESP32-C3 boards, lower the Sendspin `buffer_size` as shown
above; the default buffer does not fit in internal RAM.
