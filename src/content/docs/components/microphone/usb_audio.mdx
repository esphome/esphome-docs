The `usb_audio` microphone platform receives audio from a USB audio device, such as a USB microphone, sound card or
speakerphone, connected to the [USB Audio](/components/usb_audio/) component.

This platform only works on ESP32 variants with a USB OTG peripheral.

> [!WARNING]
> Audio and voice components consume a significant amount of resources (RAM, CPU) on the device.
>
> Crashes are likely to occur if you include too many in the same configuration. In particular, Bluetooth/BLE
> components are known to cause issues when used in combination with audio and voice components.

```yaml
# Example configuration entry
usb_audio:

microphone:
  - platform: usb_audio
    id: usb_mic
    sample_rate: 16000
    bits_per_sample: 16bit
    num_channels: 1
```

## Configuration variables

- **sample_rate** (*Optional*, positive integer): The sample rate of the audio stream, in Hz. Must be between `8000`
  and `96000` and supported by the USB device. Defaults to `16000`.
- **bits_per_sample** (*Optional*, enum): The bit depth of the audio samples. One of `8bit`, `16bit`, `24bit` or
  `32bit`. Must be supported by the USB device. Defaults to `16bit`.
- **num_channels** (*Optional*, positive integer): The number of channels, `1` (mono) or `2` (stereo). Must be
  supported by the USB device. Defaults to `1`.
- **buffer_size** (*Optional*, positive integer): The size of the buffer between the USB driver and ESPHome, in
  bytes. Must hold at least 40 ms of audio (e.g. `1280` bytes at 16000 Hz, 16 bit, mono). Defaults to `6400`, or to the minimum when that is larger.
- **usb_audio_id** (*Optional*, [ID](/guides/configuration-types#id)): The [USB Audio](/components/usb_audio/)
  component to use. Defaults to the only one in YAML.
- All other options from [Microphone Component](/components/microphone/).

Voice components such as [Voice Assistant](/components/voice_assistant/) and
[Micro Wake Word](/components/micro_wake_word/) expect 16000 Hz audio. If your USB device does not support that rate,
use a [Resampler Microphone](/components/microphone/resampler/).
